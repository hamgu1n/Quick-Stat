### A dataset of 25 salaries has one very large CEO salary added. What happens?

- The median increases a lot, but the mean barely changes

### What does it mean to say the median is "robust"?
@ the-median
- It is always larger than the mean
+ It is not strongly affected by outliers
- It uses every value in its calculation
- It only works for categorical data
> The median depends on the middle of the ordered data, so a few extreme values don't move it much.

### What does $\bar{x}$ represent?
@ the-mean
+ The mean of a sample
- The median of a sample
- The number of observations
- The most common value
> $\bar{x}$ ("x-bar") is the sample mean: the sum of the observations divided by $n$.
