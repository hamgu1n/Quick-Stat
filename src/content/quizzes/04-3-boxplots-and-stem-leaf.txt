Section Review: Boxplots and Stem-and-Leaf Plots. Format notes are in src/lib/quiz.ts.

### In a boxplot, what does the box span?
@ boxplots
- From the minimum to the maximum
- From the mean minus one SD to the mean plus one SD
- From the median to the maximum
+ From $Q_1$ to $Q_3$, the middle 50% of the data
> The box covers the IQR, and the line inside it marks the median.

### What does the line inside a boxplot's box represent?
@ boxplots
+ The median
- The mean
- The mode
- $Q_1$
> The line marks $Q_2$, the median.

### How far do the whiskers of a boxplot extend?
@ boxplots
+ To the smallest and largest values that aren't flagged as outliers
- Always to the minimum and maximum, even if they're outliers
- Exactly 1.5 × IQR from the box
- To one standard deviation from the median
> Flagged outliers are drawn as separate points beyond the whiskers.

### A boxplot shows Min = 2, $Q_1$ = 10, Median = 15, $Q_3$ = 20, Max = 26. What is the IQR?
@ boxplots
- 24
- 15
+ 10
- 5
> $\text{IQR} = Q_3 - Q_1 = 20 - 10 = 10$.

### Shop A's daily customers have IQR = 40, and Shop B's have IQR = 80. Their medians are about the same. What can you conclude?
@ boxplots
- Shop B is more consistent from day to day
- Shop B has more customers on a typical day
- Shop A has more outliers
+ Shop A is more consistent from day to day
> A smaller IQR means the middle half of the days are packed more tightly together.

### What are boxplots especially good for?
@ boxplots
- Showing every individual value
+ Comparing the distributions of two or more groups side by side
- Showing part-to-whole relationships
- Showing the relationship between two numerical variables
> Side-by-side boxplots make differences in medians, spread, and outliers easy to see.

### In a stem-and-leaf plot, how is the value 47 split?
@ stem-and-leaf-plots
+ Stem 4, leaf 7
- Stem 7, leaf 4
- Stem 47, leaf 0
- Stem 0, leaf 47
> The stem holds the leading digit(s) and the leaf holds the final digit.

### What is the main advantage of a stem-and-leaf plot over a histogram?
@ stem-and-leaf-plots
- It works better for very large datasets
+ It shows the shape of the distribution while keeping every individual value
- It shows the five-number summary directly
- It works for categorical data
> A stem-and-leaf plot preserves the original data values, which a histogram hides.

### A stem-and-leaf row reads `6 | 1 3 8`. Which values does it represent?
@ stem-and-leaf-plots
- 6, 1, 3, 8
+ 61, 63, 68
- 16, 36, 86
- 6.1, 6.3, 6.8
> Each leaf joins the stem: 61, 63, and 68 (assuming the key says 6 | 1 = 61).

### What is a back-to-back stem-and-leaf plot used for?
@ stem-and-leaf-plots
- Showing one dataset twice
- Showing the relationship between two variables
+ Comparing two groups using a shared set of stems
- Displaying categorical data
> The two groups' leaves sit on either side of the same stems, so their distributions can be compared directly.
