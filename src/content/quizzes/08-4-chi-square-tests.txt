### What type of data are chi-square tests designed for?
@ section-4-6-chi-square-tests
+ Categorical data, to compare observed counts to expected counts
- Numerical data, to compare means
- Paired before-and-after measurements
- Data from two numerical variables
> Chi-square tests work with counts in categories.

### Which describes the chi-square distribution?
@ the-chi-square-distribution
- Symmetric and bell-shaped, centered at 0
- Left-skewed with heavy tails
+ Right-skewed and never negative, with a shape set by its degrees of freedom
- Uniform between 0 and 1
> As the degrees of freedom increase, it shifts right and becomes more symmetric.

### A spinner with 4 equal sections is spun 200 times. Under $H_0$ that it's fair, what is the expected count for each section?
@ chi-square-test-for-goodness-of-fit
+ 50
- 25
- 200
- 4
> $E = n \times p_0 = 200 \times \frac{1}{4} = 50$.

### In a goodness-of-fit test, one category has $O = 60$ and $E = 50$. What is its contribution to $\chi^2$?
@ chi-square-test-for-goodness-of-fit
- 10
+ 2
- 0.2
- 100
> $(O - E)^2 / E = (60 - 50)^2 / 50 = 100 / 50 = 2$.

### A goodness-of-fit test has 5 categories. How many degrees of freedom does it have?
@ chi-square-test-for-goodness-of-fit
- 5
- 6
- 10
+ 4
> $df = k - 1 = 5 - 1 = 4$.

### A contingency table has row total 60, column total 40, and grand total 200. What is the expected count in that cell?
@ chi-square-test-for-independence
+ 12
- 100
- 30
- 24
> $E = \frac{60 \times 40}{200} = 12$.

### A contingency table has 3 rows and 4 columns. How many degrees of freedom does the test for independence have?
@ chi-square-test-for-independence
- 12
- 7
- 5
+ 6
> $df = (r - 1)(c - 1) = 2 \times 3 = 6$.

### A marketing firm asks whether age group and preferred streaming service are related. Which test fits?
@ chi-square-test-for-independence
- Chi-square goodness-of-fit test
+ Chi-square test for independence
- One-sample t-test
- Paired t-test
> Two categorical variables measured on the same people call for a test for independence.

### A candy company wants to know if bag colors match its advertised percentages. Which test fits?
@ chi-square-test-for-goodness-of-fit
- Chi-square test for independence
+ Chi-square goodness-of-fit test
- Two-sample t-test
- One-way ANOVA
> One categorical variable (color) is compared to a specified distribution.

### What condition is needed for a chi-square test to be reliable?
@ chi-square-test-for-independence
- Each observed count must be at least 30
- The data must be normally distributed
+ Each expected count should be at least 5
- There must be exactly two categories
> If expected counts are too small, collect more data or combine categories.
